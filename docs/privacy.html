Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

TeksTrader respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how TeksTrader collects, uses, shares, and keeps personal information.

This policy applies when you use the TeksTrader website, application, and related services. In this policy, these products are the “Service.”

Information We Collect

We can collect the following information:

  • Account information, such as your name, email address, username, profile details, and authentication identifiers.
  • Content that you submit, such as listings, photographs, messages, reports, and support requests.
  • Transaction details that you choose to provide, such as delivery information and payment contact details.
  • Technical information, such as your device type, browser type, IP address, app version, and activity logs.
  • Information from service providers when you use their services to sign in or interact with TeksTrader.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, or unnecessary identity documents through listings or messages.

How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service.
  • Create and secure accounts.
  • Show listings and profile information to other users.
  • Deliver messages and support user transactions.
  • Respond to questions, reports, and deletion requests.
  • Detect fraud, abuse, security incidents, and violations of our Terms of Service.
  • Meet legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

How We Share Information

Information in a public profile or listing is visible to other users and website visitors. Information in messages is visible to the message participants.

We can share information with vendors that provide hosting, authentication, storage, communications, security, and support services. These vendors can use the information only to provide their services to us.

We can also share information when required by law, to protect people or the Service, or with your direction or consent.

TeksTrader does not sell personal information.

Data Retention

We keep personal information only while it is necessary for the purposes in this policy. We can keep some information longer when the law requires it or when we need it for security, fraud prevention, disputes, or enforcement.

Deletion from active systems does not always remove information immediately from backups. Backup copies expire through the normal backup cycle.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

You are responsible for keeping your account credentials secure. Contact us promptly if you believe that another person accessed your account.

Your Choices and Rights

You can update available account information through the Service. You can request access, correction, or deletion by contacting us.

Your rights depend on the law that applies to you. We can request information to verify your identity before we act on a request.

Read our Data Deletion Instructions for the deletion process.

International Processing

Our service providers can process information in countries other than your country. Privacy laws in those countries can differ from local laws.

Children

The Service is not for people under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from people under 18.

Contact us if you believe that a person under 18 provided personal information. We will review the report and take appropriate action.

Changes to This Policy

We can update this policy when the Service or legal requirements change. We will publish the new policy and change the effective date.
